Reading Text

Effective studying is not about forcing nonstop effort. It is about matching intensity to your current energy and attention. In focus periods, try one small target at a time and remove extra tabs or notifications. In rest periods, step away, breathe, and reset before coming back to the next task.

If your attention drifts, that is data, not failure. Use short loops: read one section, summarize it in one sentence, then continue. If the section feels heavy, split it into smaller chunks and celebrate completion of each chunk.

Students with ADHD often benefit from visible structure: short timers, checklists, and kind reminders. The goal is not perfect stillness. The goal is steady progress with less friction.
